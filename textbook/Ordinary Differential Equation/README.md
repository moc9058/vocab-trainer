# Ordinary Differential Equations

Engineering mathematics course based on the six chapter pages under [Study / Engineering Math / Ordinary Differential Equations](https://app.notion.com/p/40b69428c861803c9e58eb250390d312), imported on 2026-11-01. These are structured paraphrased notes of Kreyszig, Advanced Engineering Mathematics, tenth edition, not complete textbook chapters. Each source attribution and scope statement is preserved in `md/`.

The course assumes first-year university calculus and emphasizes physical modeling and substantive mathematical problems. Full assessment boundaries and scoped examples are in [the assessment reference](curriculum/README.md); that English reference is frozen into the published curriculum and used during question generation, verification and grading.

Inline `$...$`, display `$$...$$`, LaTeX commands and matrix row separators are preserved. Notion callouts and bold/italic tags are converted to standard Markdown. The two plain-text Bessel recurrence identities in Chapter 5 are normalized to equivalent LaTeX. No omitted textbook material is reconstructed.

The manifest registers six stable chapter IDs (`ode-ch1` through `ode-ch6`) under `ordinary-differential-equations`, in the Engineering Mathematics subject. `math:sync` publishes the validated sources and curriculum separately from application deployment. New tests can select the published book; existing tests retain their pinned source versions.
